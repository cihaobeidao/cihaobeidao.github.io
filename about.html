About me

I'm a game designer who plays across PC, mobile, console, and tabletop.

At TapBlaze, I lead design on Good Pizza, Great Pizza and Good Coffee, Great Coffee. My work spans game systems, content, and live operations, with a focus on progression, economy balancing, and player experience.

What I work on

I connect player goals with game systems and content, balance economies and event rewards, and use player data and playtesting to inform design decisions.

My work also includes level design and balancing for Homecraft, collaborative design reviews, and exploring AI-assisted analysis and tools for design workflows.
